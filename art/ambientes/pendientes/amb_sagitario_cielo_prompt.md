# amb_sagitario_cielo

Prioridad 2. Referencia: `art/retratos/marisal/marisal_f1_v3.png`, solo estilo.

Use case: stylized-concept. Production art for Sal y Almas: la Odisea. Reference is ONLY painting STYLE: detailed parchment grain, fine sepia linework, golden rimlight, plum shadows, weathered materials, encrusted crystalline salt. Do not copy wolf character, ram spirit, natal wheel or portrait framing. Melancholic quiet dread through geometry and scale. No gore, blood, jump scares, text, labels, watermarks, logos or borders. Finished full-bleed landscape combat background, target 1920x1080, 16:9 composition, one scene. No foreground characters. Landmarks and detail in upper two thirds; bottom third quiet low-contrast continuous terrain or water for combat plates. Fragmentos de meteorito flotan con hierba de la estepa adherida, caminos de guijarros atraviesan el vacío y un círculo enorme de estrellas se inclina como un único ojo; horizonte curvado.. Long low horizons, nomad felt tents and meteorite craters, burnt umber grass, cobalt-plum night and cold iron starlight. A single-eyed giant guards the passage, no symbols from other projects. Architecture and dominant palette MUST be that of this biome, not the red armor from reference.
