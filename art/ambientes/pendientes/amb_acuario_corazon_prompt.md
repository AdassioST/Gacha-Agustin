Landmarks and detail in upper two thirds; bottom third quiet low-contrast continuous terrain or water for combat plates. Luz lunar opresiva.. Flat icy shelves, narrow basalt sea stacks and Nordic steep roofs, glacial pearl cyan and slate plum, pale gold aurora accents. Scandinavian and Icelandic atmosphere; distant songs lure to freezing sea. Architecture and dominant palette MUST be that of this biome, not the red armor from reference. An ENCLOSED DEEP INTERIOR, overhead enclosed vault/root/ice/rock ceiling, no open daylight landscape, outdoor horizon, mountains or sky except tiny distant entrance. Definitely below surface and visibly deeper than previous zone. Bottom third quiet.
