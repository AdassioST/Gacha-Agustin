Reference is ONLY painting STYLE: detailed parchment grain, fine sepia linework, golden rimlight, plum shadows, weathered materials, encrusted crystalline salt. Finished full-bleed landscape combat background, target 1920x1080, 16:9 composition, one scene. No foreground characters. Landmarks and detail in upper two thirds; bottom third quiet low-contrast continuous terrain or water for combat plates. El paso custodiado por el gigante: enorme arco natural en un cráter profundo, un asiento de piedra del tamaño de una colina y un arco de guerra colosal apoyado al fondo; nadie visible cerca. Luz estelar baja.. Long low horizons, nomad felt tents and meteorite craters, burnt umber grass, cobalt-plum night and cold iron starlight. A single-eyed giant guards the passage, no symbols from other projects. Architecture and dominant palette MUST be that of this biome, not the red armor from reference. An ENCLOSED DEEP INTERIOR, overhead enclosed vault/root/ice/rock ceiling, no open daylight landscape, outdoor horizon, mountains or sky except tiny distant entrance. Definitely below surface and visibly deeper than previous zone. Bottom third quiet.
