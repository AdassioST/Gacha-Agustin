# amb_piscis_cielo

Prioridad 2. Referencia: `art/retratos/marisal/marisal_f1_v3.png`, solo estilo.

Use case: stylized-concept. Production art for Sal y Almas: la Odisea. Reference is ONLY painting STYLE: detailed parchment grain, fine sepia linework, golden rimlight, plum shadows, weathered materials, encrusted crystalline salt. Do not copy wolf character, ram spirit, natal wheel or portrait framing. Melancholic quiet dread through geometry and scale. No gore, blood, jump scares, text, labels, watermarks, logos or borders. Finished full-bleed landscape combat background, target 1920x1080, 16:9 composition, one scene. No foreground characters. Landmarks and detail in upper two thirds; bottom third quiet low-contrast continuous terrain or water for combat plates. Islas de micelio flotan como nenúfares sobre un mar de nubes, cúpulas invertidas, peces de luz nadan entre hongos y dos lunas se reflejan sin aparecer en el cielo. Belleza melancólica y equivocada.. Byzantine domes and Greek colonnades softened by giant mushrooms, opal violet, deep teal and warm gold spore light. Hospitable and beautiful but time is missing; inhabitants never want to leave. Architecture and dominant palette MUST be that of this biome, not the red armor from reference.
