# amb_piscis_subsuelo

Prioridad 2. Referencia: `art/retratos/marisal/marisal_f1_v3.png`, solo estilo.

Use case: stylized-concept. Production art for Sal y Almas: la Odisea. Reference is ONLY painting STYLE: detailed parchment grain, fine sepia linework, golden rimlight, plum shadows, weathered materials, encrusted crystalline salt. Do not copy wolf character, ram spirit, natal wheel or portrait framing. Melancholic quiet dread through geometry and scale. No gore, blood, jump scares, text, labels, watermarks, logos or borders. Finished full-bleed landscape combat background, target 1920x1080, 16:9 composition, one scene. No foreground characters. Landmarks and detail in upper two thirds; bottom third quiet low-contrast continuous terrain or water for combat plates. Cavernas de micelio bajo la isla, raíces de hongos como cortinas, mosaicos sumergidos y pequeñas lámparas de sal; una escalera regresa a la misma puerta. Luz teal y lila cada vez más tenue.. Byzantine domes and Greek colonnades softened by giant mushrooms, opal violet, deep teal and warm gold spore light. Hospitable and beautiful but time is missing; inhabitants never want to leave. Architecture and dominant palette MUST be that of this biome, not the red armor from reference. An ENCLOSED DEEP INTERIOR, overhead enclosed vault/root/ice/rock ceiling, no open daylight landscape, outdoor horizon, mountains or sky except tiny distant entrance. Definitely below surface and visibly deeper than previous zone. Bottom third quiet.
