# amb_piscis_orilla

Prioridad 2. Referencia: `art/retratos/marisal/marisal_f1_v3.png`, solo estilo.

Use case: stylized-concept. Production art for Sal y Almas: la Odisea. Reference is ONLY painting STYLE: detailed parchment grain, fine sepia linework, golden rimlight, plum shadows, weathered materials, encrusted crystalline salt. Do not copy wolf character, ram spirit, natal wheel or portrait framing. Melancholic quiet dread through geometry and scale. No gore, blood, jump scares, text, labels, watermarks, logos or borders. Finished full-bleed landscape combat background, target 1920x1080, 16:9 composition, one scene. No foreground characters. Landmarks and detail in upper two thirds; bottom third quiet low-contrast continuous terrain or water for combat plates. Isla de hongos luminosos con pequeñas cúpulas bizantinas y columnas griegas entre jardines, embarcadero con barcas intactas y mesas preparadas sin comensales; luz violeta y esporas doradas.. Byzantine domes and Greek colonnades softened by giant mushrooms, opal violet, deep teal and warm gold spore light. Hospitable and beautiful but time is missing; inhabitants never want to leave. Architecture and dominant palette MUST be that of this biome, not the red armor from reference.
