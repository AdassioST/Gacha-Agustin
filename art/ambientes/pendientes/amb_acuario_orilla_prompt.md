# amb_acuario_orilla

Prioridad 2. Referencia: `art/retratos/marisal/marisal_f1_v3.png`, solo estilo.

Use case: stylized-concept. Production art for Sal y Almas: la Odisea. Reference is ONLY painting STYLE: detailed parchment grain, fine sepia linework, golden rimlight, plum shadows, weathered materials, encrusted crystalline salt. Do not copy wolf character, ram spirit, natal wheel or portrait framing. Melancholic quiet dread through geometry and scale. No gore, blood, jump scares, text, labels, watermarks, logos or borders. Finished full-bleed landscape combat background, target 1920x1080, 16:9 composition, one scene. No foreground characters. Landmarks and detail in upper two thirds; bottom third quiet low-contrast continuous terrain or water for combat plates. Archipiélago de hielo con casas nórdicas de techos inclinados, embarcaderos de basalto y barcas cubiertas de sal helada; respiraderos calientes en la orilla. Luz pálida de sol bajo sobre agua ciruela.. Flat icy shelves, narrow basalt sea stacks and Nordic steep roofs, glacial pearl cyan and slate plum, pale gold aurora accents. Scandinavian and Icelandic atmosphere; distant songs lure to freezing sea. Architecture and dominant palette MUST be that of this biome, not the red armor from reference.
